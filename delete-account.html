Delete your Motoshop POS account

Motoshop POS · Last updated: November 2026

Option 1: Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Sign in to Motoshop POS.
  2. Tap Account at the top.
  3. Tap Delete my account….
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete permanently.

If you are a shop owner, you can tap Download a backup first to save your items and sales before deleting.

Option 2: Ask us to delete it

If you cannot sign in, email YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm it is you and delete the account, normally within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Shop owner: your account, your shop, all items, all sales records, and all staff logins in your shop. This cannot be undone.
  • Staff member: your account only. Sales you made stay in the shop’s records, but your name and email are removed from them.

What may remain

Copies in system backups, if any, are removed as the backups are rotated out. Ad providers keep their own records under their own policies. See our Privacy Policy for details.
