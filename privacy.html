Privacy Policy

Motoshop POS · Last updated: November 2026

Motoshop POS is a free point-of-sale and inventory app for motorcycle shops. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. It is written in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173).

1. Who is responsible

The app is operated by YOUR FULL NAME OR BUSINESS NAME, Philippines. For any privacy question or request, email YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password. Passwords are stored in scrambled (hashed) form, and we cannot read them.
  • Shop information you enter: your shop name, the items you stock (names, barcodes, costs, prices, stock levels), your sales records (items sold, labor charges, mechanic names, shop cut, totals) and the email addresses of staff you add.
  • Who made each sale: each sale records the email of the staff member who made it, so the shop owner can review it.
  • Camera: the camera is used only on your device to read barcodes. Pictures and video are not recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as IP address, device and browser type, and time of requests, kept by our hosting provider for security and to keep the service running.
  • Data saved on your device: a login session and, in offline mode, shop data stored on your phone so the app works without internet.
  • Ads: the app is free and shows small banner ads (never on the checkout screen) served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier and similar device information to show and measure ads. You can limit or reset ad personalization in your phone’s settings.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment card details. The app records cash sales only. Please do not type your customers’ personal information into free-text fields such as job descriptions.

3. Why we use it

  • To create your account, run your shop’s sales and inventory, and compute profit and reports.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To show ads, which keep the app free.
  • To answer your questions and requests.

We use your information on the basis of your consent and of providing the service you asked for.

4. Who can see your information

  • Within your shop: the owner sees everything in the shop, including staff emails and who made each sale. Staff see only the items and the selling screen.
  • Other shops cannot see your data. Each shop’s information is kept separate.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database, login and hosting), Google AdMob (ads), and an email service that sends account confirmation emails. They process data only to provide their services.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

5. Where it is stored

Your data is stored on Supabase servers in South Korea, so it is transferred outside the Philippines. It is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is deleted as described below. Copies in system backups, if any, are removed as those backups are rotated out.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: open Account and choose Delete my account.

  • Shop owner: your shop, all its items and sales records, and the logins of all its staff are permanently deleted.
  • Staff member: your login is deleted. Past sales you made stay in the shop’s records, but your name is removed from them.

Owners can download a backup of their data before deleting. If you cannot use the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page.

8. Your rights

You have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct it, to object to or withdraw consent for its use, to have it deleted or blocked, to data portability, and to claim compensation if you are harmed by misuse of your data. To use these rights, email YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL. You may also file a complaint with the Philippine National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

9. Security

We use encrypted connections, hashed passwords and access rules that keep each shop’s data separate. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and sign out on shared devices.

10. Children

The app is for business use and is not intended for anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
